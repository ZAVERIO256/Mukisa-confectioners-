Our Favorite Recipe - Chocolate Birthday Cake

The secret recipe from MUKISA CONFECTIONARIES - Try it at home!

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 3/4 cups sugar
  • 3/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 teaspoons baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1/2 cup vegetable oil
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 cup boiling water

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch round pans.
  2. In large bowl, mix flour, sugar, cocoa, baking soda, baking powder and salt.
  3. Add eggs, milk, oil and vanilla. Beat for 2 minutes on medium speed.
  4. Stir in boiling water (batter will be thin).
  5. Pour batter into prepared pans.
  6. Bake 30 to 35 minutes or until wooden pick inserted in center comes out clean.
  7. Cool 10 minutes, then remove from pans. Cool completely.
  8. Frost with your favorite chocolate frosting and enjoy!
