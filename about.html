About Us

Who We Are

MUKISA CONFECTIONARIES is a locally owned bakery in Kampala, started 5 years ago with one small oven and a big dream. Today we bake for birthdays, weddings, Kwanjula, graduations and all celebrations in Kampala.

Our Story

I am Mukisa, the founder. I started baking birthday cakes for my neighbors in my home kitchen. Because you loved the taste, you told your friends, and now we bake over 50 cakes every week. Thank you for trusting us!
